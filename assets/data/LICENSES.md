# Data licences

`kanji.v1.json` and `strokes.v1.json` are generated by `scripts/data/build.ts` from the sources pinned in
`scripts/data/sources.json`. They are derived works and are shared under **CC BY-SA 4.0**
(the stroke paths under **CC BY-SA 3.0**). The app's code is not affected by this.

- **KANJIDIC2, JMdict, RADKFILE and KRADFILE** are the property of the
  [Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/), used in conformance with the
  Group's [licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html) (CC BY-SA 4.0). Reached through
  [davidluzgouveia/kanji-data](https://github.com/davidluzgouveia/kanji-data) (MIT),
  [@furifont/dictionary](https://github.com/antfu/furifont) (which also uses
  [JmdictFurigana](https://github.com/Doublevil/JmdictFurigana) and
  [yomitan-jlpt-vocab](https://github.com/stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab), based on Jonathan Waller's JLPT lists),
  kotobako-data (jmdict-simplified) and [@johnmorrisdotca/bushu](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@johnmorrisdotca/bushu).
- **KanjiVG** stroke data © Ulrich Apel and contributors, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/),
  <https://kanjivg.tagaini.net>.

The EDRDG asks for its data to be kept current: re-run `npm run data` after bumping the pins at least monthly.
The app shows this attribution on its About screen.

The first release of Kanjiskole: a Japanese reading trainer for kanji, kana and vocabulary, on Android and the web,
in Danish or English. Offline, no accounts, no analytics, no AI calls.

## Install (Android)

Download `kanjiskole-v1.0.0.apk` below and open it on the phone (allow installs from your browser or file manager
once). It installs over earlier test builds and keeps your progress. Check the download against
`kanjiskole-v1.0.0.apk.sha256` if you like.

Built by EAS: https://expo.dev/artifacts/eas/e15_Ulk2QwLPWatGtOFa4yq7QrBUChN9xYomE4oONTA.apk (that link expires; the
copy on this release doesn't).

## What's in it

- **Kanji**: calibration, FSRS sessions, words that only use kanji you know plus the new one, JLPT N5 → N1 with
  components first, "Hvorfor?" on every answer, kanji pages with stroke order and mnemonics, the progress map,
  Lyntest.
- **Kana**: the full chart coloured by progress, a detail sheet per character, look-alike drills, listening.
- **Vocabulary**: 12,894 words that unlock with their kanji, progress by JLPT level, search and filters, a page
  per word with furigana and sound.
- **Audio**: native-speaker recordings from Kanji alive for 4,497 words (kept offline after the first play), the
  device's Japanese voice for the rest.
- Five extra new cards beyond the daily allowance in every track; statistics per track; import and export.

Full list: `CHANGELOG.md`. Security: `docs/SECURITY-REVIEW.md` (S1–S19).
